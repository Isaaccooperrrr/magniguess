How scoring works
  • Points: 100 for a bullseye, dropping 20 for every order of magnitude you are off.
  • Tolerance: stay inside the mode's slack and you survive the question; go past it and it costs a life.
  • Streaks build on every question you survive and reset the moment you miss.
  • Answers are rounded, widely-cited figures — you are only ever graded on magnitude.
